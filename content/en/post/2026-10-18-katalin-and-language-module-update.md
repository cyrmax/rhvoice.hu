We are pleased to announce a new version of the Katalin voice and a new, improved release of the RHVoice Hungarian language module!

This update brings the latest version of Katalin along with further improvements to the Hungarian language module. Thank you to everyone who has shared feedback and helped with testing!

You can download Katalin from the [download page]({{<relref "voices">}}). For help with installation, see the [installation guide]({{<relref "installation">}}).

Try the new release and send your feedback or bug reports to [info@rhvoice.hu](mailto:info@rhvoice.hu). If you encounter a problem with how text is read, please include the affected text and tell us which platform and voice you were using.
