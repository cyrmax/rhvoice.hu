Dear friends and users of the RHVoice Hungarian beta language module,

After a long wait, we have some good news: today we released new, retrained versions of the Imre, Anna and Katalin Hungarian voices.

## What has changed?

* Updated emoji pronunciation to the latest version supported by the RHVoice core.
* Ported all Hungarian pronunciation rules and exceptions from eSpeak NG, making Hungarian text sound more natural.
* Added correct reading of dates, times, decimal numbers and ordinal numbers.
* Fixed spelling in all three voices. Short and long Hungarian vowels are now pronounced correctly when spelling out text.

This is a short list, but there are many changes under the hood for you to test. If no bugs arise during the testing period, we will publish this version as a final release, together with the source code.

## Android application update

We have also updated the beta Android application. It now supports Android Direct Boot, allowing RHVoice to speak on the lock screen after a restart, before the phone is unlocked for the first time.

You can download the voices from the [download page]({{<relref "voices">}}).
