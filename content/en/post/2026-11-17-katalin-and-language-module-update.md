We are pleased to announce a new version of the RHVoice Hungarian language module, together with a new version of the Katalin voice (4.7)!

## What has changed?

The most important change is that the language module now includes updated pronunciation exceptions for Hungarian. Exceptions are words whose pronunciation differs from the general pronunciation rules. Thanks to the updated exceptions, RHVoice pronounces more words correctly, making Hungarian text sound even more accurate and natural.

The updated language module is also included in the new installation packages of the Anna and Imre voices.

## Download and installation

You can download the voices from the [download page]({{<relref "voices">}}). For help with installation, see the [installation guide]({{<relref "installation">}}).

## Feedback

Please try the new release! If you come across a word that RHVoice still pronounces incorrectly, write to us at [info@rhvoice.hu](mailto:info@rhvoice.hu). In your report, please include the mispronounced word or sentence, how it should be pronounced, and which platform (NVDA, SAPI, Android, Linux) and voice you were using.

Thank you to everyone who has shared feedback and helped with testing!
